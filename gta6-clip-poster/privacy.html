Privacy policy: GTA6 clip poster

GTA6 clip poster is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • Data accessed: with the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to confirm which channel is signed in and whether an upload went public).
  • Storage: the OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else, and nothing is collected from viewers or other users.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads follow the YouTube Terms of Service.

Contact: through the channels' YouTube "About" page.
